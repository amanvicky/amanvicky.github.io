OpenBroadcast

Corrects your eye gaze on video calls so it looks like you're looking at the camera. 30fps on a plain CPU, no GPU. Shows up as a virtual camera in Zoom, Teams, OBS. It started when I posted a challenge on LinkedIn: who can build a CPU-only alternative to NVIDIA Broadcast's Eye Contact? This and Gazester are my answers.

PythonOpenCVMediaPipevirtual camera
→ live demo, in your browser → source

Gazester

Earlier, more research-y version of OpenBroadcast. CNN warping for the correction, MediaPipe for face tracking, and a two-person mode for demoing over LAN.

GOD MODE

A Claude skill that runs a fake software company to build your project. A PM writes acceptance criteria, rival dev squads build, a red team attacks the result, and it doesn't ship until the tests pass. First run made a working game in ~23 minutes.
